=================================================================
                 XIE'S MUSICIAN - AUTO-PLAYER & STUDIO
                      For Sky: Children of the Light
=================================================================

Author: Xie
Version: 2.0 Pro
Architecture: Single-file Portable Executable (.exe)

Features:
---------
- Minimalist, Frameless, Transparent Floating HUD & Full Studio UI.
- No taskbar icon clutter; always-on-top; quick toggle with Right-Shift.
- Anti-Ban Humanized Input (micro-jitter, chord staggering, variable hold time)
  with 1-click Toggle: ON (Mô phỏng người) / OFF (Đánh chính xác 100% theo sheet).
- Multi-Source Online Sheet Catalog (over 14,000 community sheets from vithai.xyz,
  sky-music.github.io, skysheet.store, YouTube, and Sky Community).
- Direct YouTube URL to Sky Sheet transcription with DSP pitch tracking.
- Audio (MP3/WAV/OGG) and MIDI auto-converter to 15-key Sky format.
- Automatic decryption of encrypted Sky Studio / Specy mobile exports.
- Multi-instrument synthesizers (Piano, Harp, Flute, Kalimba, Horn).
- Custom Playlists & smooth downwards scrollable library.

Hotkeys:
--------
- Right-Shift: Show / Hide Floating HUD
- F6: Play / Pause / Resume
- F8: Safe Stop / Emergency Stop
